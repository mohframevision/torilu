## Why do we write?

Torilu is a guide to places in Bahrain, and we check every place before we publish it: that it's open, that its hours are right, and that its phone number works. But a place is more than an address and opening hours. Behind every bakery are hands kneading dough before dawn, behind every small restaurant is a brave decision, and behind every dish we love is a story.

Those stories don't fit on a directory card, and that is why the articles section exists.

## What will we write about?

The topics will be many and varied, but they revolve around people and places in Bahrain:

- **People's stories:** a shop owner, a cook or a baker, how they started and what they learned along the way. We write these from real conversations with them.
- **Practical guides:** where to go for Friday breakfast, a quiet evening out, or a family outing. Every place we mention is on Torilu and has been checked.
- **Food culture in Bahrain:** the story behind what we eat and drink every day, from reliable sources.
- **Community topics:** what matters to people in their daily lives and deserves to be written about with care.

## How do we write?

We follow a few simple rules in every article:

1. **We don't make anything up.** Every piece of information comes from a visit, from the place's owner, or from a reliable source we name.
2. **We write clearly and warmly,** as if telling a friend, not filing a report.
3. **We put accuracy before speed.** An article may take longer, but it is never published incomplete.
4. **We correct our mistakes.** If you find something inaccurate, tell us through the [contact page](/en/contact.html) and we'll fix it.

## Have a story?

If you know a place with a story worth telling, or you own a place and would like us to visit and hear from you, write to us through the [contact page](/en/contact.html).
