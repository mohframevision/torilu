<section class="page-header">
  <h1>Community delivery: DD Station</h1>
  <p>A Bahraini community initiative, an alternative to delivery apps.</p>
</section>

<article class="prose">
<p class="dd-note">A free information page. Torilu does not run this service or work with it, and takes no payment. The initiative started on 25 November 2026.</p>

<h2>What is the idea?</h2>
<p>Delivery apps charge restaurants fees and commissions, so your order costs more. DD Station suggests another way: you order directly from the restaurant at the pickup price, and a driver from your area delivers it for a fixed fee.</p>
<p>The drivers are students who need an income, or young people looking for work. Each area has a group of drivers and a coordinator.</p>

<h2>How does it work?</h2>
<ol class="dd-steps">
  <li>Order directly from the restaurant at the pickup price.</li>
  <li>Send the invoice number and your location to the coordinator of your area's group.</li>
  <li>The driver collects your order and delivers it. You pay the driver the fee.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Why do we show it?</h2>
<p>Because the idea helps both customers and young people in Bahrain. The founder owns the restaurant Five Spoon, which takes part in the initiative.</p>
